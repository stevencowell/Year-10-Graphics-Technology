Use feedback, write the capstone and select folio evidence.

Four-module pathway

One connected Year 10 course

1

Term 1

Graphic Design & Communication

Use visual conventions, vector construction and design principles to communicate responsibly for an audience.

  • Safety signs and visual conventions
  • Vector graphics and Illustrator workflows
  • Design principles, audience, culture and intellectual property
Open Module 1
2

Term 2

Architectural Drawing

Move from spatial research to coordinated architectural views, then justify building-context decisions without overstating compliance.

  • Spatial planning and architectural research
  • CAD drafting, drawing views and conventions
  • Sustainability, building context and professional roles
Open Module 2
3

Term 3

Engineering Drawing

Select projection systems, preserve design intent in CAD and release only the technical claims supported by controlled evidence.

  • Projection systems and freehand communication
  • CAD modelling, materials and manufacturing
  • Dimensioning, standards, assemblies and animation
Open Module 3
4

Term 4

Student Negotiated Project

Define an approved graphics problem, manage its development and evaluate the final communication through evidence.

  • Brief, audience, criteria and constraints
  • Ideation, project management and digital development
  • Presentation, feedback and evaluation
