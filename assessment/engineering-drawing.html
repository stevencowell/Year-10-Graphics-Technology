What is currently confirmed

The official Year 10 Assessment Booklet 2026 schedules a formal Engineering Drawing task for Term 4 Week 3, weighted at 30%, assessing GT5-1, GT5-2, GT5-3, GT5-4, GT5-5, GT5-6, GT5-11 and GT5-12.

Task outline and required evidence

Current approved 2026 detail: Teacher to confirm The authorised 2025 notice stated the end of Term 4 Week 4 and included GT5-7. The current schedule controls: Term 4 Week 3 and the outcome list above. The possible evidence below is historical draft grounding only.
  • Research and theory on engineering roles, materials, drawing terminology, CAD/CAM and drawing conventions.
  • Freehand isometric sketches based on teacher-issued engineering examples.
  • Fully dimensioned CAD drawings based on teacher-issued examples.
  • Assembly or exploded-view animations where required.
