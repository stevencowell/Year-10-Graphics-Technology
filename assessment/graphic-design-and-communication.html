What is currently confirmed

The official Year 10 Assessment Booklet 2026 schedules a formal Graphic Design and Communication task for Term 1 Week 10, weighted at 35%, with outcomes listed as All.

Task outline and required evidence

Current approved 2026 detail: Teacher to confirm The following possible evidence comes only from the authorised 2025 teacher notice. Use it for planning discussion, not as the final 2026 instruction.
  • Five workplace-sign graphics: first aid, mandatory personal protective equipment, no smoking, exit and warning.
  • Annotated concept sketches for the five signs.
  • A presentation of the signs.
  • Short reports on composition, proportion, balance, tone and contrast.
  • A report on respectful local Aboriginal and/or Torres Strait Islander consultation.
  • A report on trademarks and copyright.
