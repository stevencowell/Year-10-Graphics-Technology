What is currently confirmed

The official Year 10 Assessment Booklet 2026 schedules a formal Architectural Drawing task for Term 2 Week 8, weighted at 35%, assessing GT5-1, GT5-2, GT5-3, GT5-4, GT5-5, GT5-6, GT5-9, GT5-10, GT5-11 and GT5-12.

Task outline and required evidence

Current approved 2026 detail: Teacher to confirm The authorised 2025 notice stated Term 3 Week 2, which conflicts with the current schedule. Term 2 Week 8 above is controlling. The possible evidence below is historical draft grounding only.
  • Research and proposed changes for a residential renovation concept.
  • A CAD drawing set that may include a site plan, floor plans, elevations, a section and a roof plan.
  • Research on building context and sustainability.
  • One landscape-design or interior-design extension.
  • Research on the roles of architects, interior designers and engineers.
  • An outline of residential building stages.
  • WHS risk-management responses relevant to graphics work.
